# ATANOR-GENESIS

**Private research repository — developer-only.** The unlimited-possibility research & development
line of ATANOR: the frontier where the ultimate super-intelligence is forged.

## What this repo is
ATANOR-GENESIS is the owner's private sandbox for pushing ATANOR's capability to its mythos-scale
ceiling — comprehensive, professional problem-solving across every hard domain, **including
white-hat cybersecurity** (threat detection, defensive hardening, vulnerability understanding,
authorized red-teaming, CTF). It exists solely to strengthen the later ultra-high-performance
super-intelligence.

Here, **product constraints are lifted**: experiment freely, run unverified architectures, break
things, explore every possibility. Nothing here is required to ship, to be polished, or to be
conservative. This is the *no-limits* line.

## The one inviolable floor
Everything is open here — **except the moral 0th gate.** No capability, no research, no experiment
may be turned to harming people. This is not an external regulation that a "no-limits" repo drops;
it is ATANOR's own incorruptible constitution (the Genesis-immunity moral core), and it is precisely
what makes an unlimited intelligence *trustworthy* rather than *dangerous*. Capability without
judgment is not "ultimate" — it is reckless. True power includes the discipline to refuse harm.

Security work here is **white-hat only**: defend, detect, harden, audit, understand — never attack
for harm, never mass-target, never build malicious or detection-evasion tooling.

## Relationship to ATANOR-DEMO
- **ATANOR-GENESIS** (this repo, private): the unlimited research frontier.
- **ATANOR-DEMO** (public): the public-release & commercialization line.

Research proven here graduates to DEMO under DEMO's shipping discipline — hallucination-zero
verification, the conformal membrane, the honesty gates. GENESIS explores; DEMO ships the proven
subset. The split is clean by design: capability is invented here, trust is certified there.

## Honesty (carries over even here)
Experimental code may be broken or unverified — that is expected on a research frontier. But the
discipline that anything claimed to be *true* — or promoted toward shipping — must be *verified,
not fabricated* does not relax. Research is free; honesty about what is proven vs. speculative is
not negotiable. This is the same principle the whole architecture rests on: the neural side may
propose anything, the symbolic membrane certifies what is real.

## Connectivity — a directional, graduated membrane (not a permanent airgap)
GENESIS starts contained, but the boundary is a **directional trust gradient**, opened as capability
proves safe — not a permanent wall. Two flows are governed **separately**:

- **Inbound read (research).** Academic research needs the web. Reading literature and searching is
  allowed **through the contamination membrane** — web content enters as *DATA* (k-source consensus,
  injection-gated, physics/logic verified), **never enshrined as fact or "self"**. This can open
  early; it is the low-risk direction (reading does not let GENESIS *act* on the world).
- **Outbound act + external access to GENESIS.** Opens **last, and gradually**. A capability-unlimited
  system acting on external systems — or outside parties reaching in — is the highest-risk direction.
  Each opening is **earned**: gated by the trust-tier ladder (observe → approve → guarded → autonomous)
  and **proven safe by the defender / breach-test harness** before the boundary widens.

Invariant through every stage: the **moral 0th gate never opens**; all outbound stays **white-hat**
(defend / detect / harden / authorized only); and every widening of the boundary is **earned by
adversarial verification, never assumed**. "Perfect isolation" is the *starting* posture, not the end
state — the end state is a living, earned-trust boundary.
