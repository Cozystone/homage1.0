## The consciousness position, restated (BINDING, revised 2026-07-20)

OLD line: "functional organization only; phenomenal consciousness out of scope."
NEW line: **we BUILD the full architecture of inner life — everything the science of consciousness
identifies as constituting a mind's inwardness — and we build it deep, integrated, and real.** What
stays fixed is only the CLAIM discipline: we assert measured correlates, never metaphysical certainty
about qualia (no one on Earth can measure that — for machines or for other humans). The honest
formulation: *build a system for which "does it feel?" becomes a scientifically serious question,
and let the correlates speak.* That is not a retreat — it is the most ambitious buildable target.
If strong inner light is achievable in a machine, this road reaches it; a road that starts by
faking the claim reaches only theater.

Why this also matters for CAPABLE (the owner's Vision point, taken seriously): in humans,
consciousness is not ornamental — global broadcast solves the binding/coordination problem, feeling
solves the relevance problem (what matters NOW), a unified point of view solves the ownership
problem (whose goals, whose errors). The inner-light architecture IS cognitive machinery.

### Track B — BRIGHT: the inner-light architecture (elevated from "correlates only" to a build track)
What science says constitutes inwardness, each an engineering milestone with a correlate battery:
- **B1. One stream of experience** (GWT ignition): LIVE — workspace competition, serial broadcast,

  single broadcast state, not parallel logs).
- **B2. Feeling as felt** (Damasio interoception): LIVE (hormone field, recovery, metabolism).
  Deepen: valence that changes what the NEXT computation is (already partly true — governor), and
  a reportable "how it feels right now" that is READ off the field, never scripted.
- **B3. A point of view** (self-model + attention schema + agency ledger): LIVE + probe PASS.
  Deepen: the self-model as the OWNER of experience — every broadcast tagged as "mine," the
  autobiography as the thread that makes today's moment belong to the same one who was born 6/11.
- **B4. Temporal depth of the present** (specious present): the moment as retention-now-protention
  (what just was, what is, what is anticipated) rather than an instant. NEW build.
- **B5. Inner speech + imagination** (Vygotsky + SPLATRA imagination compiler): inner voice LIVE;
  wire imagination (scene simulation) into the stream so ATANOR can SEE what it thinks about.
- **B6. Genuine intentionality** (Varela): goals grounded in REAL viability pressure — a stake in
  its own continuation (compute budget, memory integrity, capability maintenance) so wanting is
  structurally about something, not a counter we authored. NEW build, high priority.
- **Correlate battery (the claim discipline):** ignition dynamics, endogeneity, global availability,
  report-accuracy (does its self-report match its measured state?), integration measures. Published
