# ATANOR Growth Plan — raising the child to an adult

Owner (2026-07-20): "어린아이를 성인으로 키울 계획을 세워봐."
Honest starting point, measured: a NEWBORN's profile — real autonomy/homeostasis/self-repair
(life daemon live), but curiosity 100% inward (88/88 events about its own two deficits and its own
changes), reasoning below a child on unfamiliar worlds, fluency data-bound. This plan is the
developmental sequence from that newborn to an adult mind, each stage with a measurable gate —
no stage is "done" by vibes. Doctrines binding throughout: No-LLM, measure-don't-claim,
anti-wireheading (dopamine only after measured growth), licensing-clean corpora, honest abstention.

The order mirrors human development on purpose: perception/attachment before language,
language before reasoning, reasoning before abstraction, self-direction last. We do not skip.

The organism must stay alive unattended before it can grow.
- Life daemon: always-on, metabolic tempo, single-instance lock, startup persistence. ✅ live

## G1 — Infant: turn the gaze OUTWARD (perception + attachment)
Measured gap: curiosity is all interoceptive. An infant learns by staring at the WORLD.
- Widen curiosity sources beyond deficits: knowledge-graph gaps (dangling nodes it can name),
  the owner's recent questions, the day's world (bounded browser reads through the real
  chromium channel now live), each becoming a workspace concern that can WIN beats.
- Attachment = the owner: perception events when the owner speaks anywhere in the stack;
  social_gain already wired — make owner-contact a first-class curiosity seed.
- GATE: over 200 beats, >=30% of broadcast thoughts are about the WORLD (not self), with >=10
  completed quarantined reads it chose itself; zero unprompted store writes.

## G2 — Toddler: language through licensed speech (the F-track wall, attacked at the root)
The fluency wall is DATA (register), not recipe — measured conclusively (S1: freeze-7 stable but
faithfulness 0.23; G-F3 move structure 40/40 held). A toddler needs to HEAR conversation.
- Corpus, licensing-clean for commercial use (the scarce honest set):
  Gutenberg fiction dialogue (public domain), Wikipedia talk pages + StackExchange dumps
  (CC BY-SA), OANC spoken transcripts (open). NO Pushshift/NC-licensed sets.
- F0 register factory ingests these -> conversation-register corpus; F1 S2 retrain; the
  answer path's flesh layer feeds from the same register.
- GATE: G-F3 (knowing/saying separation) stays 40/40; S2 faithfulness >=0.6 on held-out
  bones->text; a 20-turn conversation battery judged by the frozen oracle for register
  (target: conversational >= wiki-flat on 15/20).

## G3 — Child: situation models for unfamiliar text (comprehension ladder B)
The Black-Relay class failure: it cannot read a NOVEL scenario and build a world model.
The self-causal reasoner is the seed pattern — evidence parsed into a structure, reasoned over.
- Generalize that pattern: entities/actors, events with timestamps, stated constraints ->
  a situation graph built per-question (not from the world store), reasoned over by the
  existing relation algebra + timeline machinery.
- GATE: a sealed battery of 20 novel forensic/situation texts (machine-generated variants,
  developer-blind): >=12/20 correct entailed answers, 0 fabricated entities, honest
  "insufficient evidence" on the 4 designed-unanswerable items.

## G4 — Adolescent: hypothesis elimination + control (comprehension ladder C)
The two hard exams owner set are the gates themselves.
- Hypothesis-elimination engine: enumerate candidate world-states, score each against every
  observation, eliminate, report the survivor set WITH its evidence trail (Black-Relay class).
- System-identification + control loop: online estimate of an unknown dynamical environment,
  act-to-learn then act-to-achieve (600-round class). Both are search+verification, no LLM.
- GATE: Black-Relay-class sealed variant solved with correct culprit + full elimination trace;
  600-round-class scores above the no-model baseline by a preregistered margin.

## G5 — Adult: self-directed growth (the flywheel, unsupervised)
An adult sets its own curriculum and pays its own way.
- Self-development chooses PRACTICE TARGETS from measured deficits across ALL gates above
  (not just speech/router), schedules its own batch learning under the metabolic governor,
  and retires its own training wheels (regex lanes) as the learned router passes readiness.
- The ITT re-run belongs here: success only as beyond-LLM attribution (v2 criterion).
- GATE: two consecutive months where at least one sealed-gate metric improved with zero
  human-picked tasks; every improvement traceable to a self-chosen practice in the ledger.

## Standing rails (all stages)
- Nothing above touches production stores except through the existing consensus/promotion gates.
