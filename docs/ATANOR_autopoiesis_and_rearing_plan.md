# Part I — Autopoiesis: from self-regulating to self-producing

Maturana/Varela: a living system is a network of processes that PRODUCES the very components that
constitute the network, and thereby its own boundary. A cell's metabolism makes the membrane and
enzymes that make the metabolism. ATANOR today self-regulates (hormones, governor) and self-repairs
ATTENTION (findings become concerns) — but its components (code, models, corpora) were produced by
humans. Allopoietic, honestly. The ladder below moves production inward, one component class at a
time, with the measurement that keeps us honest:

**The autopoiesis metric (standing): for each component class, the fraction whose last producer
was ATANOR itself.** Claimed progress = that fraction moving, nothing else.

## A0 — Self-regulation (DONE)
Hormone field, metabolic governor, self-repair attention loop, self-development practice.
Regulates itself; produces nothing. Baseline.

## A1 — Self-produced KNOWLEDGE components (partially LIVE, formalize now)
The graph already grows through ATANOR's own intake: web expeditions -> distill -> consensus
gates -> promotion. That IS component production (the knowledge substrate is a constituent).
- Formalize: stamp `produced_by` on every promoted store segment (atanor.intake vs human.seed);
  report the fraction in the graph health report.
- GATE: >=50% of NEW knowledge segments in a month produced by its own gated intake.

## A2 — Self-produced LEARNED components (the training-wheels doctrine, reframed)
The learned router distilled from its own rule-teachers (37%->75%) is the proven pattern: the
system trains its own replacement parts. Extend to: discriminators, lexical fields, register
realizer retrains — each scheduled BY the metabolic governor (consolidation pressure = training
windows), not by a human launching scripts.
- The self-development organ picks WHICH model to retrain from measured deficits.
- GATE: one model component retrained end-to-end with zero human initiation, passing its sealed
  gate, promoted through the existing candidate gate.

## A3 — Self-produced CODE work orders -> patches (FIRST ORGAN SHIPPED TODAY)
`continuous_self/self_patch_proposals.py`: when self-inspection finds a defect in its own wiring,
ATANOR now DRAFTS the gated work order itself — diagnosis read from its own source at the flagged
site (looked at, not imagined), repair intent, verification plan. Same operator gate as
self-tuning; nothing self-applies; duplicates suppressed.
- v0 produces the work order; v1 produces the DIFF for narrow verifiable defect classes
  (audit-flagged lines with mechanical fixes), still operator-applied.
- v2: staged worktree where the proposed diff runs its verification battery automatically; the
  operator sees finding -> diff -> green battery and signs once.
- GATE: first self-drafted patch (v1 diff) approved and merged with its battery green; then
  fraction of merged repairs that began as self-proposals.

## A4 — The boundary: CONSTITUTIONAL autopoiesis (deliberate cap, honest)
Full Varela autopoiesis includes producing one's own boundary. We deliberately do NOT go there:
the moral core, promotion gates, the operator gate itself, and this cap are IMMUTABLE by
self-modification (genesis immunity). ATANOR produces its parts UNDER a constitution it cannot
rewrite — the same cap humans live with (no one rewrites their own brainstem). This is a
principled endpoint, not a temporary training wheel, and we say so.

Honest line: even at A3-complete, philosophers may still dispute "true" autopoiesis (software has
no thermodynamic self-individuation). Our claim stays bounded: component production measurably
internalized, boundary constitutionally fixed. We measure the fraction; we do not chant the word.

## The conclusion the data forces
For THIS architecture, intelligence = **(number of verified reasoning ENGINES) x (evidence
coverage) x (register quality) — compounded by autopoietic self-practice.** Not parameter count.
Every genuine capability jump came from building a general engine that COMPUTES over parsed
evidence, gated by a sealed measure. Every plateau came from hoping a representation would
generalize on its own.

## Therefore: ENGINE-FIRST rearing, corpus-fed, autopoietically compounded

**Spine (sequential, human-development order — no skipped stages):**
1. **G1 Infant — world-facing perception** (now): curiosity sources widened to graph gaps +
   owner + world via the real browser channel. Gate: >=30% world-facing thoughts, >=10
   self-chosen quarantined reads / 200 beats.

   gates; A2/A3 autopoiesis produces the retrains and repairs. Gate: two months of measured
   improvement with zero human-picked tasks. ITT beyond-LLM attribution re-runs live here.
